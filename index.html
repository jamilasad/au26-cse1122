What went wrong, most often

From your 38 lab submissions and 15 quiz questions. If one of these is you, it is on your card below.

12Wrong logic, or echoed the input

The commonest failure by far — several programs read the number and printed it straight back without computing anything. Read the problem, then write the steps on paper before you type.

5Did not compile at all

A missing semicolon, an undeclared variable, an invented function. Always compile and run once yourself before submitting.

2Integer division printed 0

int a, b; then b/a gives 0, not 0.6. For a decimal one side must be double, or cast it: (double)b/a.

29%scanf and the &

The weakest topic in the whole quiz. %d for int, %lf for double, and an & before every variable. Forget the & and it often still compiles, then behaves madly.

33%What the verdicts mean

Only a third could say what AC, WA, PE, CE and TLE actually mean. You cannot fix what you cannot read — we will do this together in class.

17Fully correct

Seventeen of you passed every hidden test — nearly half the class writing correct, correctly-formatted code in week two. Keep going.
